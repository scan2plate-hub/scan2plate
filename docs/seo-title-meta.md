# Title and meta description rewrite (SEO phase 3)

**Last updated:** 2026-09-21  
Generated when phase 3 was applied. Verified by `node scripts/seo-audit.mjs`:
every title is 50–60 characters, every description 150–160, every page has
exactly one `<h1>`, and Open Graph and Twitter tags mirror the page rather
than the homepage.

## Why the old ones failed

Titles ran as short as 29 characters (`About Scan2Plate | Scan2Plate`), which
wastes roughly half the line Google will give you and says the brand twice
while saying the product once. Descriptions were mostly feature lists with no
benefit and no call to action. Several pages carried the homepage's Open Graph
title, so every share of any page looked like a share of the homepage.

## Headings

Six headings appeared identically across 21–22 pages: `Quick answer`,
`How it works`, `Benefits`, `FAQ`, `Related Scan2Plate pages` and
`Ready to review Scan2Plate for your restaurant?`. A heading is a scanning
and ranking signal; repeating the same six on every page tells a reader and a
search engine that these are the same page.

Each is now page-specific — `How QR ordering at a table works`,
`What offline billing changes day to day`. The `Quick answer` headings were
deleted outright: phase 1 removed the paragraphs they introduced, so they
were labelling nothing.

## Not done here

Body copy is phase 4. These pages still average 336 words and 78 of 91 page
pairs remain above 30% similar — a good title on a thin, duplicated page
still ranks badly. The five new URLs in the keyword map do not exist yet and
get their titles when they are written.
