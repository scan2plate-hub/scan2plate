Offline Restaurant POS Software That Bills Without Internet

Scan2Plate is restaurant billing software that keeps working when the internet does not. The desktop and Android apps store each bill on the device during an outage, give it a unique ID, and upload it automatically when the connection returns. Billing, KOT printing and bill printing continue throughout. It costs ₹499 a month.

Why an outage costs a restaurant money

A power cut, a dead broadband link or a mobile tower under load does not pause the dinner rush. Tables still order, the kitchen still cooks, and guests still want to leave.

When a cloud-only billing system loses its connection, a restaurant falls back to a notebook. That works for ten minutes. Over an hour it produces a pile of handwritten slips that somebody has to key in later, usually after midnight, usually with mistakes. Bills get missed. GST totals stop matching the day's cash.

The risk is worst exactly when it hurts most: Friday and Saturday evenings, when the network is busiest and so are you.

How billing through an internet outage works

  1. The app caches your menu. While you are online, the installed app keeps a local copy of your menu, prices, tax rate and table list, so it has everything it needs before a connection is ever lost.
  2. The connection drops. Billing does not stop or show an error. You keep taking orders on the same screen.
  3. Each bill is stored on the device with its own ID. Every offline bill gets an identifier in the form OFF-<your restaurant ID>-<date>-<unique code>, generated on the device. That ID is what makes the next step safe.
  4. KOT and bill printing continue. The kitchen ticket prints and the customer bill prints, because both come from the device and its printer, not from a server.
  5. The connection returns and sync runs. The app compares the bills waiting on the device against the bills the server already has, and uploads only the ones that are missing.

That last step matters more than it sounds. Because the comparison is by ID, a sync that is interrupted halfway — the connection drops again, someone closes the app — simply resumes. A bill that already reached the server is never uploaded twice. You do not end up with the same ₹840 table counted twice in the day's total.

The totals themselves are calculated by the same code on desktop, on Android and on the website, and that is verified by an automated test on every change. A bill with 5% tax and a ₹30 discount comes to the same figure whether it was raised offline on a laptop or online in a browser.

What works offline, and what does not

Being straight about the limits is more useful than a longer list. Anything that needs to reach a customer's phone or a payment gateway needs the internet, because those things are not on your device.

Who this suits, and who it does not

It suits you if your area has unreliable power or broadband, you run a full floor at peak hours, or you have ever had to bill into a notebook and key it in afterwards. Restaurants like Old Monk Cafe in Darbhanga run on a connection that is good most of the time and occasionally is not — which is the normal case, and the case this is built for.

It suits you less if your restaurant is entirely QR-order-driven with no counter billing at all. Guests ordering from their own phones need the internet on their side, and nothing installed on your device can change that. If that is your whole model, offline mode protects your billing but not your order intake.

Questions restaurant owners ask about offline billing

Do I lose bills if the app closes during an outage?

No. Each bill is written to the device as it is raised, not held in memory, so closing the app or restarting the machine does not lose it. When you reopen the app and the connection returns, the pending bills are still there and sync uploads them.

Can the same bill be uploaded twice and inflate my sales total?

No. Every offline bill carries an ID generated on the device, and sync compares those IDs against what the server already holds before uploading anything. A bill that has already arrived is skipped. An interrupted sync resumes from where it stopped rather than starting again.

Does the GST amount calculate correctly offline?

Yes. Tax and discount are calculated on the device using the same formula as the online system, from the tax rate cached with your menu. An automated test compares both implementations on every change, so an offline bill and an online bill for the same items total the same.

Does offline mode cost extra?

No. It is part of the ₹499 per month subscription, like every other Scan2Plate feature. There is no higher tier that unlocks it and no separate desktop licence. Install the app on as many devices in your restaurant as you need.

What happens to QR orders placed during an outage?

Guests cannot place a QR order while your internet is down, because their phone reaches Scan2Plate over the internet rather than over your device. Staff can take those orders at the table and bill them offline as normal. QR ordering resumes on its own once the connection is back.
