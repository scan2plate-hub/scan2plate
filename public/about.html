Our Mission

Scan2Plate is built to make restaurant digitization simpler for Indian food businesses. The goal is to help owners manage service, billing, kitchen visibility, inventory, payments, and reports without replacing the character of their restaurant.

Who We Serve

The platform is designed for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, hotels, food courts, and street-vendor style counters that need practical digital workflows for dine-in, takeaway, pre-order, token, and table ordering.

Product Purpose

Scan2Plate connects customer ordering, staff order handling, kitchen preparation, billing, payment tracking, inventory updates, and analytics so teams can work with clearer information during busy hours.

What Scan2Plate changes day to day

  • Clearer order flow from customer to kitchen
  • Faster billing and payment status tracking
  • Better owner visibility into menu, stock, and sales
  • Configurable workflows for different food-service formats

How how Scan2Plate started works

  1. Set up business details, menu, tables or service type, tax, payments, and staff access.
  2. Customers or staff place orders through QR menu, counter, table, token, or pre-order workflows.
  3. Kitchen and admin teams manage KOT, preparation, billing, payment status, inventory, and reports.

Questions restaurant owners ask about Scan2Plate

Who builds Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate is built in India for Indian restaurants, by a small team that works directly with the restaurants using it. Feature decisions come from what owners hit during service rather than from a roadmap written in advance. Old Monk Cafe in Darbhanga was among the first restaurants running it.

Why is everything one price?

Because tiered pricing works by putting something a restaurant will eventually need behind a plan it is not on yet, which makes the headline figure meaningless. One price at ₹499 a month means the cost on day one is the cost in month twelve, and no feature can be moved into a higher tier later.

Is Scan2Plate suitable for a single restaurant?

Yes, that is what it is built for. A single restaurant or a small group run by the people who own it is the intended case. Large chains needing head-office control across many branches are better served elsewhere, and the pages here say so rather than leaving it to be discovered.

Where is Scan2Plate used?

Across India, by restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens and other food businesses. Pricing is in rupees, GST and KOT are built in rather than adapted, and the offline apps exist because Indian connectivity made them necessary.
