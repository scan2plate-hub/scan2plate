Common Questions

Which restaurants use Scan2Plate?

Old Monk Cafe in Darbhanga is a named reference. We do not publish customer counts, because a number nobody can check is not evidence. Where a restaurant has agreed to be named and has described what changed, it appears here.

Can I speak to an existing customer?

Ask through the contact page. Whether an introduction is possible depends on the restaurant agreeing, which is theirs to decide rather than ours. A working session on your own menu usually answers more than a reference call does.

Why are there so few case studies?

Because we only publish ones we can stand behind, with the restaurant's agreement and without invented figures. A page of unverifiable testimonials is easy to write and worth nothing to a buyer who is paying attention.

What should I ask a reference?

What broke in the first week, how long staff took to get comfortable, and what they still do outside the software. Those three answers tell you more than a list of features any vendor will confirm.
