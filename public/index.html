What is Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate is an all-in-one restaurant billing software, QR ordering system, kitchen display, inventory, analytics, pre-order, and restaurant management software for India.

Who should use Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate is built for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, hotels, food courts, and vendors that need restaurant POS software, QR menu software, table ordering, kitchen display, billing, stock tracking, and reports from one web platform.

How does Scan2Plate work?

Restaurants add menu items, tables, billing settings, payment details, kitchen workflows, and inventory records. Customers scan QR codes or place pre-orders, staff receive live orders, kitchens update preparation status, and owners review billing and analytics.

Features

  • Restaurant billing app with bills, taxes, payment status, and printable receipts.
  • Restaurant QR ordering, digital menu software, and table ordering system for dine-in guests.
  • Kitchen display system and KOT workflow for preparation visibility.
  • Restaurant inventory software with stock, supplier bills, and low-stock alerts.
  • Restaurant analytics for sales, orders, payments, and menu performance.
  • Pre-order support with advance or full payment and table selection.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is restaurant billing software?

Restaurant billing software helps restaurants create bills, calculate taxes, track payments, print receipts, and connect billing with kitchen and reporting workflows.

What is QR ordering?

QR ordering lets customers scan a table or counter QR code, open a mobile digital menu, add items, and place orders without waiting for a paper menu.

How is Scan2Plate different from traditional POS?

Traditional POS tools often focus on counter billing. Scan2Plate combines billing, QR ordering, table ordering, digital menus, KOT, inventory, analytics, pre-order, and restaurant automation.

What are the benefits of Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate can reduce manual order errors, improve kitchen visibility, speed up billing, support restaurant QR ordering, track inventory, and give owners clearer sales analytics.
