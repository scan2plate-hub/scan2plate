Questions restaurant owners ask about these guides

What do these guides cover?

Practical questions Indian restaurant owners actually ask: what a KOT is, how GST applies to restaurant bills, how to work out food cost percentage, and whether QR ordering suits a particular floor. They are written for people running a service, not for search engines.

Are these guides only about Scan2Plate?

No. Most cover how something works in a restaurant regardless of which software runs it. Where a guide mentions Scan2Plate it says so plainly, and where a competitor does something differently that is stated rather than avoided.

How often is new writing published?

New guides are added as questions come up often enough to be worth answering properly. A thin post published on a schedule is worth less than a thorough one published when it is ready.

Can I suggest a topic?

Yes. If something about running a restaurant keeps costing you time and there is no clear answer online, tell us through the contact page. Questions from people actually running restaurants make better guides than keyword research does.
