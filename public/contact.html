Questions restaurant owners ask about getting in touch

How do I get a demo?

Book one from the demo page and we will show Scan2Plate running on your own menu rather than a sample one. A demo takes about twenty minutes and covers billing, QR ordering, the kitchen screen and reports. Nothing is charged before you decide.

Can I ask questions before booking anything?

Yes. Pricing, whether a specific workflow is supported, and what moving from another system involves are all reasonable things to ask first. Being told a feature does not exist before you buy is more useful to both sides than finding out afterwards.

How quickly do you respond?

We aim to reply to sales and support enquiries on the same working day. If something is broken during service, say so in the first line of the message so it is picked up before general enquiries.

Do you help with setup?

Yes. Getting the menu, tables, tax rate and staff logins in is the bulk of the work when starting, and we will walk through it with you. Most restaurants are billing on the same day they begin setting up.
