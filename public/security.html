Common Questions

What is Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate is restaurant management software for billing, QR ordering, kitchen display, inventory, analytics, pre-order workflows, and daily restaurant operations.

Who should use Scan2Plate?

Restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, hotels, food courts, and street food vendors can use Scan2Plate when they need faster ordering, billing, KOT, and reporting.

How does Scan2Plate work?

A restaurant sets up menu, tables, billing, kitchen, payment, and inventory settings. Customers scan QR codes or place pre-orders, while staff manage orders from the admin and kitchen dashboards.

What are the benefits?

Scan2Plate reduces manual order errors, speeds up billing, improves kitchen visibility, supports QR menus, tracks inventory, and gives owners clearer sales analytics.
