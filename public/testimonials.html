Common Questions

Are these reviews verified?

Only named restaurants that have agreed to appear are shown. We do not publish anonymous testimonials or aggregate star ratings, and there is no review schema on this page, because a rating nobody can verify is not evidence and marking it up as one is against search engine guidelines.

Why are there not more?

Because we publish what restaurants have actually agreed to say. Filling a page with unattributed praise takes an afternoon and persuades nobody who is paying attention. Old Monk Cafe in Darbhanga is a named reference.

Can I leave feedback?

Yes, through the contact page, including when something is not working. Criticism from a restaurant mid-service is more useful than praise, because it names the thing that needs fixing.

How should I evaluate Scan2Plate?

Run it on your own menu for one quiet service alongside whatever you use now, and compare the day-end totals. That tells you more in one evening than any testimonial page can.
