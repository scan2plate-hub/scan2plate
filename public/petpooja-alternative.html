What actually differs between restaurant systems

Most comparisons list features both products have. That is not where the decision sits. Four things separate restaurant systems in practice, and they are worth checking on any vendor you shortlist, including this one.

  1. What the price includes. A low headline rate matters less than which features sit behind a higher tier. Ask which plan includes the kitchen display, the inventory module and the reports you actually intend to use.
  2. What happens when the internet fails. A cloud system with no installed app stops billing when the connection does. Ask for a demonstration with the network switched off, not a description of it.
  3. What it costs to leave. Ask about contract length, notice period, and whether you can export your own menu, bills and customer data without asking permission.
  4. Who answers when it breaks at 9pm. Support hours matter more than a feature list when a bill will not print mid-service.

When Petpooja is the better choice

Petpooja is an established vendor with a large installed base in India, and there are situations where that is the deciding factor.

Choose the incumbent if you depend on a specific third-party integration — a particular aggregator, accounting package or payment terminal — and it is already supported there. A larger vendor has had longer to build those connections, and a missing integration is a daily cost that no price difference makes up for.

Choose the incumbent if you run several branches with complex central control, or you need on-site support in a city where they have staff and we do not. Scan2Plate is built for a single restaurant or a small group run by the people who own it.

Choose the incumbent if your team is already trained on it and working well. Switching billing systems costs a week of friction during service. That is worth paying only when the thing you are switching for is worth more than the week.

What switching actually involves

  1. Export your menu. Get your item list, prices and categories out of your current system before you cancel anything.
  2. Set up in parallel. Configure Scan2Plate with your menu, tables, tax rate and staff logins while the old system is still running. Nothing is switched off yet.
  3. Run one quiet service on both. Bill a weekday lunch on Scan2Plate and check the day-end total against the old system. A mismatch is far cheaper to find on a Tuesday than a Saturday.
  4. Move the floor over. Print new table QR codes, brief the staff, and run a full service.
  5. Keep the old account for one cycle. Do not cancel until you have a full month of reports you trust.

Step three is the one people skip and regret. A billing system that is 2% wrong is worse than one that is obviously broken, because you will not notice for a month.

Questions restaurant owners ask about switching from Petpooja

Will I lose my old bills if I switch?

Your historical bills stay in whichever system created them, so export what you need for accounts before you close the account. Scan2Plate starts your records from the day you begin billing on it and does not import past transactions from another vendor.

How long does switching take?

Setting up the menu, tables, tax rate and staff logins is the bulk of the work, and depends on menu size rather than on the software. Running both systems in parallel for one quiet service before moving the floor over is the step worth not skipping.

Is ₹499 the full price, or does it rise later?

₹499 per month per restaurant is the price, and every feature is included at it. There is no higher tier that unlocks the kitchen display or inventory, and no per-feature add-on. The pricing page lists what that covers.

Can I try it before cancelling my current system?

Yes, and you should. Book a demo, set Scan2Plate up alongside what you run now, and bill a real service on it. Keep the old subscription until you have a month of reports you trust. Switching under pressure is how mistakes reach the accounts.
