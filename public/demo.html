Questions restaurant owners ask about a free demo

What happens in a demo?

We run Scan2Plate against your own menu and show the flows you actually use: taking an order, sending the kitchen ticket, printing a bill, and reading the day's report. It takes about twenty minutes and is a working session rather than a slide presentation.

Does a demo cost anything?

No. The demo is free and there is no commitment afterwards. You do not need to cancel an existing system or provide payment details to see Scan2Plate running.

Can my staff join the demo?

Yes, and it helps. The people who will bill during a rush are better placed than an owner to say whether a flow is fast enough. Bringing whoever runs the counter usually surfaces the questions that matter.

Can I try it on real orders afterwards?

Yes. Most restaurants set Scan2Plate up alongside their current system and bill one quiet service on it before switching the floor over. Keeping the old system until you have reports you trust is the sensible order.
