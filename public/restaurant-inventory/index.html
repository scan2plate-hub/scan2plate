Quick answer

Scan2Plate supports restaurant inventory workflows so owners can understand stock, purchases, usage, and low-stock situations.

Stock Management

Track ingredients, purchase entries, supplier information, available stock, and low-stock signals.

Bill OCR and Auto Inventory

Where configured, bill import and OCR workflows can help reduce manual entry and connect purchasing records to inventory updates.

Usage and Purchases

Inventory visibility helps restaurants compare item usage, purchases, and daily operations.

FAQ

What is Scan2Plate?

Scan2Plate is restaurant billing, QR ordering, digital menu, KOT, kitchen display, inventory, table management, payment tracking, analytics, and restaurant management software.

Who is Restaurant Inventory Software for?

It is for restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, hotels, food courts, and vendor-style food businesses that need practical digital ordering, billing, kitchen, and reporting workflows.

How does Scan2Plate work?

Restaurants configure menus, tables, QR links, billing, payment, kitchen, inventory, and staff workflows. Customers or staff place orders, kitchens update preparation, and owners review bills and reports.
