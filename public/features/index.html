Questions restaurant owners ask about every feature

Is any feature limited to a higher plan?

No. Billing, QR ordering, digital menu, KOT, kitchen display, tables, inventory, staff and reports are all included at ₹499 a month. There is no higher tier, so nothing on this page can be moved out of reach of what you are paying.

Do I have to use all of it?

No. Most restaurants start with billing and the menu, then add inventory and staff records once the daily flow is settled. Everything is available from the first day at the same price, so the order you adopt things in is yours to pick.

Which features need an internet connection?

QR ordering and online payments need the internet on the guest's side, and the live kitchen screen needs a connection. Counter billing, KOT printing and bill printing continue during an outage on the installed desktop and Android apps.

What is not included?

Recipe-level food costing, centralised multi-branch control, delivery aggregator integration, and GST return filing. Scan2Plate produces the figures returns are built from but does not file them.
